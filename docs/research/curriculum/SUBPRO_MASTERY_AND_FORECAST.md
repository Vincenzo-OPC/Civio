# Subprofessional mastery and preparation priorities

21 October 2026. This is a proposed learning protocol, not a score guarantee, an implemented tutor, or a measured diagnosis of the founder. Aim for exceptional independent performance. The initial concern about fractions identifies where to investigate, not a conclusion that all mathematics is weak. [Evidence and official scope](CSC_COMPETENCY_GAP_MAP.md) govern.

## Diagnose before assigning remediation

Conduct two short, private sessions. Begin untimed, unaided and without a calculator. Ask for a brief method or estimate before showing choices on selected items; capture confidence and whether help was used. Never show diagnostic answers in advance.

| Numerical diagnostic block | Items | What to distinguish |
|---|---:|---|
| Arithmetic, multiplication/division, order and estimation | 4 | Computation versus reading/setup errors |
| Fraction meaning, equivalence, comparison, operations | 6 | Part-size misunderstanding versus algorithm slip |
| Decimals and percentage part/base/reversal | 4 | Place value versus wrong reference quantity |
| Ratio, substitution and equation translation | 4 | Unit-part reasoning versus word-order reversal |
| Work, speed, elapsed time, measurement and means | 4 | Model selection versus arithmetic |
| Mixed unfamiliar numerical transfer | 2 | Whether a familiar concept is recognized in new wording |
| **Numerical total** | **24** | Screening, not a reliable 24-point mastery scale |

Add a separate 24-item breadth screen: six English, six Filipino, six clerical (filing and spelling), six GI distributed across the four official areas. These small samples route follow-up diagnostics; they cannot establish that a subject is mastered. Do not add the Professional analytical subtest to a Subprofessional score.

After an error, ask the learner to explain the step, then give two fresh probes of the suspected prerequisite. One wrong answer does not prove a misconception. Record `not enough evidence` when interpretation is uncertain. A correct guess is not demonstrated understanding. Treat slow accurate work as a fluency question, not a low-intelligence label.

The ten new draft items are authoring examples, not an approved diagnostic form. This session has exposed their answers; they must not serve as a clean founder holdout. A separate reviewer selects and withholds the evaluation form from the teaching workflow.

## Prerequisite sequence and teaching method

Use numerical nodes N01-N22 in the [blueprint](data/competency-blueprint.json). Move past demonstrated strengths. The core dependency is arithmetic and equal groups -> fraction meaning -> equivalence/common units -> operations -> decimals/percentages -> ratio and elementary algebra -> varied rate/measurement problems -> mixed transfer. Elapsed time can be taught alongside arithmetic; there is no need to postpone it until advanced algebra. Means build on totals and division. Sequences depend on arithmetic patterns, not mastery of all word problems.

A 35-50 minute session can use 5 minutes of retrieval, 10 minutes of one worked explanation and a guided problem, 15-20 minutes of independent practice, and 5-10 minutes of mixed retrieval and error reflection. This is a starting schedule, not a validated optimal dose. Increase or shorten based on fatigue and available time. Build confidence from successful reasoning, not easy repeated scores.

For every worked solution, write: **asked quantity -> given information -> applicable concept -> steps with units -> independent check -> why each tempting wrong answer fails**. Remove scaffolding gradually. Teach a second route when it reveals meaning, not simply to display a shortcut.

Example teaching explanation, separate from the assessment pool: if a quantity changes from one fraction of the same whole to another, the added amount corresponds to the difference of those fractions. First rename them using equal-sized parts. Then find one part and scale to the whole. Check that the starting amount plus the addition equals the final fraction. This addresses the Vera tank and fractional-equation skills without teaching a memorized tank formula.

Rate teaching starts with output per unit time: one job in t days means 1/t job per day. Add compatible rates, not completion times. For staffing, count worker-hours only when equal hours, equal rates and divisible work are stipulated. For percentages, name the base before calculating; a second percentage usually acts on a changed base.

## Mastery, retention and time

| Stage | Evidence needed | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation | Can explain the concept and identify a counterexample | Guided examples |
| Guided practice | Can complete steps as prompts are removed | Fresh unaided problems |
| Independent practice | Correct setups and checks across different representations | Add modest timing |
| Timed fluency | Accuracy stable under a relevant time budget | Mixed unfamiliar problems |
| Transfer | Solves unseen contexts without a supplied formula | Delayed fresh assessment |
| Retention | Correct unaided performance after a delay | Wider spacing and periodic mixed practice |

Proposed routing heuristic, requiring pilot calibration: consider a skill provisionally secure after at least 8/10 first-exposure unaided answers over two days, followed by at least 4/5 on a delayed fresh probe about a week later, including transfer tasks. This is a practice rule, not a scientific mastery probability. For the founder's exceptional-performance goal, continue until errors are rare across several fresh mixed sets and can be explained and corrected. A 10/10 set alone is weak evidence: even a 95% Wilson interval has a lower bound near 72% with only ten successes. Never display 100% readiness.

Review intervals of roughly 1, 3, 7, 14 and 28 days are a workable initial schedule, adjusted by performance. Reuse the installed FSRS scheduler when its persistence and review queue are properly implemented. Schedule concepts with fresh transfer items as well as selected repeated facts. Repeated-item accuracy is useful retention evidence but must be labeled separately from unfamiliar performance.

Time evidence is valid only for comparable difficulty, reading load and assistance. Keep active thinking time separate from interruptions, background tabs, retries and wall-clock exam time. Do not rank learners by speed or shorten target time merely because a repeated item became familiar. Use all four outcomes: fast/correct -> fresh challenge; slow/correct -> fluency practice; fast/wrong -> check guessing or a stable misconception; slow/wrong -> inspect prerequisite and comprehension. These are hypotheses, not diagnoses.

## Preparation-priority forecast

| Priority | Evidence class | Recommended emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| All published shared subjects plus Subprofessional filing/spelling | Confirmed official competency | Cover every area; exclude no official area based on reviewer popularity |
| Fraction meaning, ratios, percentage base, translation and unit reasoning | Evidence-supported educational priority | High prerequisite value and founder concern; confirm with diagnostics |
| English/Filipino comprehension, sentence structure and paragraph coherence | Official competency plus observed bank-depth need | Read unfamiliar passages and justify answers from textual evidence |
| Clerical accuracy under explicit rules | Official Subprofessional competency | Spelling patterns, indexing and comparisons with careful checking |
| Constitution, ethics, rights and environment applications | Official competency | Source-backed scenarios as well as precise factual recall |
| Particular future question, exact topic share or predicted score | Low-confidence speculation | Do not forecast numerically or buy supposed leaked material |

Use existing 145-question Subprofessional mocks and their current internal blueprint initially, explicitly labeled as Civio practice coverage. Keep difficulty and family composition comparable across measured attempts. Recheck official timing and scope for the actual sitting. The official general rating threshold does not turn a Civio percentage-correct score into a CSC rating.

Begin with short mixed sessions before full mocks. Once foundations are stable, use an unseen timed mock periodically, review errors the next day, and adjust the next week from the error pattern. Do not exhaust clean holdouts by taking daily repeated full mocks. Maintain two or more separated evaluation forms where the reviewed bank allows them; report shortages honestly.

## Founder-first private evaluation

Use the founder's existing authenticated account and an owner-scoped opt-in pilot setting when engineering later implements it. No public learner data, email addresses, actual scores or personal weaknesses belong in research commits. Keep draft content in isolated preview storage; production practice continues to use approved content only. Experimental learning tools must never reveal protected mock keys or modify scores.

For the first two weeks, log task completion, unclear wording, explanation usefulness, wrong setups, independent accuracy, median correct-answer time and help needed. Compare fresh equivalent tasks at baseline and follow-up, with a delayed check. This single-person pilot evaluates usability and instructional response; it does not establish a product-wide effectiveness claim. Founder access may include all pilot tools, while scoring and question correctness remain identical for everyone.
