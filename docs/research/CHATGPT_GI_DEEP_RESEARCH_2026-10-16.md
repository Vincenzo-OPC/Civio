## Important source distinctions for reviewers

1. **Independent office is not the same as an Article IX Constitutional Commission.** CSC, COMELEC and COA are in Art. IX-A, §1; the Commission on Human Rights is established by Art. XIII, §17, with investigative functions in §18. Do not count CHR among the three Article IX commissions.
2. **Impeachment initiation and trial are different powers.** The House has the exclusive power to initiate cases (Art. XI, §3(1)); the Senate tries and decides cases (Art. XI, §3(6)). In question distractors, do not interchange them.
3. **Money-bill origination differs from amendment.** Art. VI, §24 assigns exclusive origination to the House; the Senate may propose or concur with amendments. Do not claim that the Senate cannot amend.
4. **Judicial-review and executive powers are separate.** Art. VIII, §1 includes review of grave abuse of discretion. The President's executive power derives from Art. VII, §1.
5. **Civil-service rules are not identical to rights for all private employees.** Article IX-B, §2 establishes civil-service merit, security of tenure and campaigning limits. Do not extend every personnel rule to unrelated private employers.
6. **A citizen-letter reply duty is not the same question as completing an application.** R.A. 6713, §5(a) expressly requires a response within **15 working days**, stating action taken. Other statutes and issuances govern specific government-service processing times; do not merge these deadlines without checking them.
7. **R.A. 6713 disclosure provisions contain scope and exceptions.** Section 8 specifies filing periods, categories of covered personnel, and information within the declaration. The 30-day assumption and separation obligations and April 30 annual deadline are statutory; more recent administrative instructions should be checked before any user-facing real-world compliance advice.
8. **Divestment has two different periods.** R.A. 6713, §9 references 30 days for resigning from a covered private-business position and 60 days for divesting the relevant interest, tied to assumption of public office, with specified exceptions.
9. **Waste responsibility is divided by level.** R.A. 9003, §10 places biodegradable/compostable/reusable collection at the barangay level, and non-recyclable and special-waste collection with the city or municipality. Do not make every waste type exclusively a barangay responsibility.
10. **Water pollution coverage is broader than the primary emphasis.** R.A. 9275, §3 primarily addresses land-based pollution but applies water-quality management across water bodies and enforces standards irrespective of pollution source.
11. **Air-pollution liability and water quality are distinct.** R.A. 8749, §2 identifies the polluter-pays principle; §§3–4 recognize prevention, public information and participation.
12. **Climate adaptation is not mitigation.** R.A. 9729, §3 defines adaptation as adjustment to climate impacts; greenhouse-gas reduction is a separate mitigation theme. Avoid calling flood-protection measures automatically a reduction in emissions.
13. **Wildlife conservation is broader than parks.** R.A. 9147 covers wildlife and habitats, regulates collection and trade, and contains allocation of jurisdiction between DENR and DA. The two written practice items concern policy, not specific permit approval.
14. **As-written source law is not always complete current legal advice.** Administrative rules, amendments, jurisprudence and exceptions may change application. These are study items and require a section-by-section expert review before release.
