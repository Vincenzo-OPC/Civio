## Statute check (R.A. 6713, Lawphil text)

- **0111 and 0112 → §4(A)(c).** Justness and sincerity says officials shall not extend undue favors on account of office to relatives by consanguinity or affinity, except appointments to strictly confidential posts or coterminous personal staff. A spouse on a promotion panel, and a cousin recommended for a regular vacancy, are that favor. They are not §7(a), which is a financial or material interest in a transaction the office must approve. Both stems now say the post is not confidential and is not coterminous personal staff, so the exception is not a second key.
- **0119 → §7(a), not §9.** The member would take a commission from a bidder on a contract the committee must approve. That is a direct financial interest under §7(a). §9 is the 30/60-day divestment clock that runs from assumption of office. It is named in the Reveal so the two sections are not mixed.
- **0120 → §7(b)(1).** The evaluator accepts paid work as a consultant to a company the office licenses. That is the bar on employment as a consultant in a private enterprise licensed by the office. It is not §7(c), which is misuse of confidential information.
- **0122 → §7(c).** Copying confidential bid prices onto a personal drive to help a relative price a bid is using confidential information for a private advantage. The old §4 label was too broad.
