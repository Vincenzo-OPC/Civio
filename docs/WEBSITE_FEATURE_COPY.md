## Short description

Practice Civil Service Exam questions with timed mocks, drills, and explanations. Civio is an independent reviewer, not the Civil Service Commission.

## Groups

### Practice

Full mocks and focused drills.

Professional is 150 questions and 3 hours 10 minutes. Subprofessional is 145 questions and 2 hours 40 minutes. Study Mode lets you check an explanation. Exam Mode waits until you submit.

### Learn

Explanations and mistake review.

After you answer, you can open an explanation. Review Mistakes keeps a question you missed. A later correct answer, given without the explanation, clears it. One clear does not finish the question.

### Improve

Saved progress and practice history.

Your finished mocks stay in History. Analytics shows practice counts. It does not predict the exam.

### Study anywhere

A lighter layout and offline drills.

Lite Mode is for a slow connection. Some topic drills can be downloaded and answered offline. The server checks those answers when you sync. Smart Review needs a connection.

## Smart Review

Practice again when it matters.

Civio reminds you to try a missed question again before you forget it. It is for a signed-in learner. It does not run offline.

Do not say "FSRS" on the public site. Do not say a question is mastered.
